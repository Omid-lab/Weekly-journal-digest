💡 Rest Day Recommendations

Days 6-7: Active Recovery

  • Skillrow or Excite Synchro — 20-30 min steady, low intensity. Both are non-impact and move blood through the legs without adding leg fatigue.
  • Skillmill in unpowered walk mode — 20 min easy. Save the sled and sprint modes for days you are not lifting hard.
  • Excite Recline if your legs are wrecked from Day 3 or Day 5 — seated, fully unloaded.
  • Mobility and foam rolling for 15-20 minutes
  • Focus on sleep quality (7-9 hours)

Recovery Tips

  • Consume protein within 2 hours post-workout
  • Stay hydrated: 3-4 liters of water daily
  • Manage stress with meditation or deep breathing
  • Take a deload week every 4-6 weeks
